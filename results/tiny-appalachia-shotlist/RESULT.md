# "Tiny Appalachia" — 30-clip shot list from real West Virginia places

**Task:** `tasks/tiny-appalachia-shotlist/TASK.md` — 30 real, recognisable WV subjects rendered as tilt-shift miniature dioramas, each with a house-style text-to-video prompt and a no-copyright sound idea, plus a 30-day posting order. All reference links retrieved and checked **2026-10-05**. Subjects are described in original words; follow the links for the sources' own detail.

**House style (applies to every prompt below):** tilt-shift miniature diorama · shallow depth of field · slow camera push-in or gentle orbit · 6 seconds · vertical 9:16 · soft natural light · cozy handcrafted-model look · tiny figures/vehicles for scale · **no readable text, no logos**. Series rules from the brief: cozy, nostalgic, respectful — real places, no stereotypes, no caricatures of the people who live there.

**Sound sources used throughout (all free-to-use; check each track's licence — some need attribution in the description):**
- **Pixabay Music & Pixabay sound effects** — free under the Pixabay Content License: https://pixabay.com/music/
- **Free Music Archive** — tracks under Creative Commons licences (filter to CC0/CC-BY): https://freemusicarchive.org
- **YouTube Audio Library** (inside YouTube Studio) — free, many tracks attribution-free
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)** — CC-BY 4.0, attribution required: https://incompetech.com
- **Freesound** — field recordings and ambience, CC0/CC-BY per clip: https://freesound.org
- Your own phone field recordings (a river, a porch, rain on a metal roof) beat stock for this series — free forever and unmistakably local.

## The 30 subjects, prompts and sounds

### 1. Babcock State Park — Glade Creek Grist Mill · Fayette County
- **Why it's loved:** routinely billed as West Virginia's most photographed place; the mill looks like it was built to be a postcard.
- **Best depiction:** autumn, late morning — red and gold foliage around the mill, creek running full.
- **Recognisable details:** weathered wooden clapboard mill on a stone foundation; big overshot waterwheel with its flume; boulder-strewn creek and small falls in the foreground.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Babcock State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babcock_State_Park)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a weathered wooden grist mill beside a rocky creek, large overshot waterwheel turning slowly, stone foundation, small waterfall over boulders, red and gold autumn trees, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural morning light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** creek water and waterwheel creaks (Freesound, CC0) under a soft solo fiddle cue (Pixabay Music, "folk" tag).

### 2. Harpers Ferry — The Point and Lower Town · Jefferson County
- **Why it's loved:** two rivers meet at a mountain water gap below a town that climbs its own hillside — geography and history in one frame.
- **Best depiction:** autumn, morning — peak colour mid-October to early November; mist on the rivers.
- **Recognisable details:** the Potomac and Shenandoah merging at the Point; preserved 19th-century stone-and-brick Lower Town terraced up the hill; the small brick engine house (John Brown's Fort); Jefferson Rock above.
- **Reference:** [NPS — Lower Town guide](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/learn/historyculture/map-and-guide-to-the-lower-town.htm)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a tiny historic river town where two wide rivers join at a rocky point, stone and brick storefronts climbing a steep hillside, small brick engine house near the shore, autumn trees on the ridges, river mist, shallow depth of field, gentle camera orbit, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural morning light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** river ambience and distant birdsong (own recording or Freesound CC0); a few soft banjo plucks (Pixabay Music).

### 3. Cass Scenic Railroad · Pocahontas County
- **Why it's loved:** century-old geared steam locomotives still shove open passenger cars up a logging railroad's switchbacks, past a preserved company town.
- **Best depiction:** fall foliage, late September to mid-October, midday.
- **Recognisable details:** black Shay locomotives with vertical cylinders and exposed side drive shafts, *pushing* open flatcars; rows of white former company houses; Shay No. 5 (1905) is the state steam locomotive.
- **Reference:** [WV Encyclopedia — Cass Scenic Railroad](https://wvencprod.wvnet.edu/entries/959)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a black geared steam locomotive with exposed side drive shafts pushing open passenger cars up zigzag mountain switchbacks, tiny white company houses in the valley, red and orange fall forest, puffs of white steam, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in along the track, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural daylight, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** steam hiss and a lonesome whistle (Freesound CC0 train recordings) — the train *is* the music; add nothing else.

### 4. Lewisburg — Washington Street · Greenbrier County
- **Why it's loved:** a National Register main street of 18th- and 19th-century brick and stone, once voted America's coolest small town — and it still feels like one.
- **Best depiction:** autumn or spring, golden hour on the brick façades.
- **Recognisable details:** brick and stone storefronts along Washington Street; the Old Stone Presbyterian Church; the white-columned General Lewis Inn; Carnegie Hall.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Lewisburg, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewisburg,_West_Virginia)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a small-town main street at golden hour, brick and stone storefronts, a stone church, a white-columned inn, tiny strolling figures and parked cars, warm window light, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in down the street, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural evening light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** gentle street ambience — footsteps, a screen door, distant church bell (Freesound CC0) with a soft piano cue (YouTube Audio Library).

### 5. Seneca Rocks · Pendleton County
- **Why it's loved:** a pale stone razorback rearing out of a green valley — the state's signature climbing wall and roadside gasp.
- **Best depiction:** spring–fall; autumn valley colour, late-day light on the fins.
- **Recognisable details:** grey-white Tuscarora quartzite fins — twin North and South Peaks split by a central notch — rising ~900 ft over Seneca Creek; tiny coloured dots of climbers on the face.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Seneca Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seneca_Rocks)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of pale grey-white rock fins with a notch between twin peaks rising above a green river valley, tiny climbers as coloured specks on the rock face, small farm fields below, shallow depth of field, gentle camera orbit, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural late-afternoon light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** wind through pines and a red-tailed hawk's cry (Freesound CC0); silence-forward, no music.

### 6. Dolly Sods Wilderness · Tucker County (edges into Randolph/Grant)
- **Why it's loved:** a windswept, tundra-like plateau that looks borrowed from northern Canada — the state's strangest landscape.
- **Best depiction:** autumn — heath barrens turn red; also spring wildflowers.
- **Recognisable details:** wind-flagged red spruce with branches only on the leeward side; boulder fields and the Bear Rocks sandstone outcrops; bogs and open barrens.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Dolly Sods Wilderness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Sods_Wilderness)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a windswept high plateau, small spruce trees bent one way by the wind, grey boulder fields and sandstone outcrops, red autumn heath barrens, low drifting cloud, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural overcast light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** open-plateau wind and meadow birds (Freesound CC0) under a sparse dulcimer-style royalty-free cue (Pixabay Music).

### 7. Mountain State Forest Festival — Elkins · Randolph County
- **Why it's loved:** since 1930, Elkins has crowned a queen (Maid Silvia) outdoors at peak colour — WV's grandest fall pageant, with lumberjack contests for ballast.
- **Best depiction:** the festival itself — roughly nine days spanning late September into the first week of October (2026 coronation: Friday, October 2).
- **Recognisable details:** outdoor coronation at the Davis & Elkins College amphitheatre with 40 princesses in gowns; Grand Feature and firemen's parades; wood-chopping contests; peak fall colour everywhere.
- **Reference:** [Mountain State Forest Festival](https://www.forestfestival.com/88th-mountain-state-forest-festival-maid-queen/)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a small mountain town fall festival, outdoor amphitheatre ceremony with rows of tiny figures in colourful gowns, a parade of tiny fire trucks on the main street, wood-chopping contest in a park, red and gold trees, shallow depth of field, gentle camera orbit, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural afternoon light, festive cozy model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** crowd murmur, marching-band drums from a distance (Freesound CC0), brass-free royalty-free parade cue (YouTube Audio Library).

### 8. Thurmond · Fayette County
- **Why it's loved:** a near-ghost town in the New River Gorge whose main street is a railroad track — five residents, a 1904 depot, and a century of coal money gone quiet.
- **Best depiction:** October foliage; early-morning river mist.
- **Recognisable details:** the two-storey C&O depot (now an NPS visitor centre and Amtrak flag stop); a single row of brick commercial buildings facing the tracks instead of a street; a single-track bridge over the New River.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Thurmond, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurmond,_West_Virginia)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a tiny near-ghost railroad town in a river gorge, a two-storey depot and a row of brick storefronts facing railway tracks, a small diesel train passing slowly, morning mist over the river, autumn hillsides, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural dawn light, quiet cozy model look, no readable text, no logos."

### 9. Shepherdstown — German Street · Jefferson County
- **Why it's loved:** West Virginia's oldest town (chartered 1762), with a main street that kept its 19th-century manners — iron fences, mounting blocks, brick sidewalks.
- **Best depiction:** autumn colour; soft morning light down the street.
- **Recognisable details:** Federal-style brick houses and storefronts on German Street; brick sidewalks and mature trees; limestone bluffs dropping to the Potomac.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Shepherdstown Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepherdstown_Historic_District)

- **Sound:** small-town morning — sparrows, a bicycle bell, café cups (Freesound CC0); gentle acoustic cue (Pixabay Music).

### 10. Blackwater Falls · Tucker County
- **Why it's loved:** a 57-foot waterfall the colour of strong tea, pouring into a hemlock gorge — and in winter it freezes into sculpture.
- **Best depiction:** autumn foliage; winter freeze as the alternate; nearby Lindy Point is a noted sunset.
- **Recognisable details:** amber/tea-coloured water (tannin-stained by hemlock and red spruce) over a sandstone ledge; boardwalk and long staircase to the viewing decks.
- **Reference:** [WV State Parks — Blackwater Falls](https://wvstateparks.com/park/blackwater-falls-state-park/)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of an amber-coloured waterfall pouring over a rock ledge into a deep green gorge, wooden boardwalk and staircase with tiny visitors, hemlock and autumn trees, drifting spray, shallow depth of field, gentle camera orbit, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural afternoon light, cozy handcrafted model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** the waterfall itself, front and centre (Freesound CC0); no music — let the water carry it.

### 11. Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum — Weston · Lewis County
- **Why it's loved:** one of the largest hand-cut stone buildings in the world, a Kirkbride landmark saved from demolition and now Weston's anchor attraction. Depict with respect — it is a place of real history, not a haunted-house gag.
- **Best depiction:** October, dusk (tours run April–October; autumn evenings suit its gravity).
- **Recognisable details:** blue-grey hand-cut sandstone; staggered "bat-wing" Kirkbride wings stretching ~1,300 ft; central clock tower; hundreds of windows.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Allegheny_Lunatic_Asylum)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a vast blue-grey hand-cut stone building with staggered wings and a central clock tower at dusk, warm light in a few windows, autumn trees on a broad lawn, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural twilight, dignified quiet model look, no people close-ups, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** evening crickets and a distant clock chime (Freesound CC0) with a very soft ambient piano (YouTube Audio Library). No horror stingers.

- **Recognisable details:** COR-TEN weathering-steel single arch (1,700 ft span) in rust brown; the deck filled with tiny pedestrians on Bridge Day; parachutes blooming below the deck.
- **Reference:** [NPS — New River Gorge Bridge](https://nps.gov/neri/planyourvisit/nrgbridge.htm)

### 13. Spruce Knob · Pendleton County
- **Why it's loved:** at 4,863 ft it is the roof of West Virginia — a stone tower above one-way spruce trees and endless layered ridges.
- **Best depiction:** late spring through fall; October colour; clear nights for stars.
- **Recognisable details:** stone-and-steel observation tower; wind-flagged spruce (krummholz) along the Whispering Spruce Trail; 360° ridge views.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Spruce Mountain (West Virginia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spruce_Mountain_(West_Virginia))
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a small stone observation tower on a mountain summit, one-way bent spruce trees, layered blue ridges fading to the horizon, first stars appearing at dusk, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural twilight, serene cozy model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** summit wind and silence (own recording) under a spacious ambient cue (Free Music Archive, CC0/CC-BY).

### 14. Green Bank Telescope · Pocahontas County
- **Why it's loved:** the world's largest fully steerable radio telescope — a gleaming white 100 m dish, taller than the Statue of Liberty, listening to the universe from a deliberately quiet valley.
- **Best depiction:** clear nights (a famous astrophotography subject); autumn hillsides by day.
- **Recognisable details:** the huge white offset dish (100 × 110 m) tilting toward the sky; green Allegheny valley; the National Radio Quiet Zone setting — no town clutter.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Green Bank Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Bank_Telescope)
- **Prompt:** "Tilt-shift miniature diorama of a giant gleaming white radio telescope dish in a green mountain valley at night, the dish tilting slowly, a sky full of stars and the Milky Way overhead, tiny science buildings with dim red lights, shallow depth of field, slow camera push-in, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft starlight, wondrous cozy model look, no readable text, no logos."
- **Sound:** near-silence — crickets and a soft synth-pad ambient cue (Pixabay Music, "space ambient"). The quiet is the joke and the truth: it's a Quiet Zone.

### 15. Mothman Statue — Point Pleasant · Mason County

- **Best depiction:** dusk/night, when the metal and red eyes read best; the Mothman Festival runs the third weekend of September.
- **Recognisable details:** muscular winged humanoid statue (Bob Roach, 2003); bat-like wings; Main Street setting at the Ohio–Kanawha confluence town.
- **Reference:** [Wikipedia — Mothman Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mothman_Festival)

- **Sound:** small-town night ambience and a theremin-free, plucked-string "mystery" royalty-free cue (Incompetech, CC-BY — light, not scary).

### 16. Blenko Glass Company — Milton · Cabell County

### 17. Berkeley Springs State Park · Morgan County
- **Why it's loved:** George Washington bathed here; the warm springs (a constant 74.3 °F) still run through a park in the middle of town.
- **Best depiction:** year-round — a winter soak in the steam is the signature shot; summer adds the spring-fed pool.
- **Recognisable details:** the white 1815 Old Roman Bath House; the stone "George Washington's Bathtub" and open spring channels through the downtown park; Berkeley Castle's golden sandstone turrets on the ridge above (exterior view only — access is uncertain, see Unsure).
- **Reference:** [WV State Parks — Berkeley Springs](https://wvstateparks.com/park/berkeley-springs-state-park/)

- **Recognisable details:** tapered steel tower with a lamp room and wraparound gallery deck; 122-step spiral staircase inside; the lake marina below.
- **Reference:** [Summersville Lake Retreat — Lighthouse](http://www.summersvillelakeretreat.com/)

## How I checked it

Every subject's county, signature details, season and reference link were verified against web search results on 2026-10-05 (three parallel verification passes, 10 subjects each, using official sites, NPS/USFS, WV State Parks, WV Tourism and Wikipedia). All 30 URLs above are taken verbatim from those search results — none were constructed. Calendar anchors were validated by weekday arithmetic: Bridge Day's third Saturday of October 2026 is Oct 17 (Day 12), and Day 26 lands on Oct 31 for the Pumpkin House. Where a verifier could not confirm a detail from source text, it is flagged below instead of asserted.

## Unsure about

- **Dolly Sods county:** the wilderness sits almost entirely in Tucker County with small sections in Randolph and Grant — listed as Tucker (primary).
- **Fiesta (Hancock Co.) and Kenova Pumpkin House (Wayne Co.):** the towns (Newell, Kenova) and all visual details were confirmed; the county names are standard attributions that the retrieved snippets did not state explicitly.
- **Seneca Rocks "time of day":** no source names a signature time; late-day light is photographer's convention.
- **Blennerhassett's sternwheeler name** (*Island Belle*) was confirmed only via a visitor blog, so the prompt just says "sternwheeler."
- **Berkeley Castle access:** sources conflict (privately owned vs. foundation tours) — the Berkeley Springs prompt shows it only as a distant exterior on the ridge, which is safe either way.
- **Summersville Lighthouse colour:** sources confirm the steel tower, lamp room and beacon but not a paint colour, so neither caption nor prompt asserts one.
- **Blenko Festival of Glass timing** rests on secondary mentions; the shot list only claims weekday blowing demos as the dependable draw.
- Festival dates move slightly year to year; the 2026 dates used (Bridge Day Oct 17, Fasnacht Feb 14, Strawberry Festival May 9–17, Forest Festival coronation Oct 2) are the editions found in sources retrieved 2026-10-05 and should be re-checked before the winter/spring "preview" posts run.
