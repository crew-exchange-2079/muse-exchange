# US Mobile $10/month Unlimited Starter promo (FBF10) — eligibility, port-in steps, true cost, catches

Read 2026-10-17, from US Mobile's own public pages only (no login, no account, no purchase). **Verdict up front:** worth it for a household that has a real wireless number to port in before Oct 20 and is happy with the Starter plan — $10/month, taxes and fees included, for 6 months, then $25/month. It is **not** available for a brand-new number, and a VoIP/Google Voice number is excluded.

## 1. Exact eligibility (quoted from US Mobile's official pages)

From the plans page banner (https://www.usmobile.com/plans):

> "FLASH BLACK FRIDAY #FBF — $10 monthly Starter w/ code FBF10 — **Port-ins for 6 mos. Number brokers & VOIP services excluded. Limit 5 per household. Renews at full price.**"

From the Unlimited Starter plan card on the same page:

> "New line offer — $10 /mo. Use code FBF10. **Port-ins only. Renews at $25/mo. after 6 mos.** Terms apply."

From US Mobile's official promo archive (https://www.usmobile.com/promo-archive):

> "FlashBlack Friday (#FBF) — Start Date: October 15, 2026. End Date: **October 20, 2026**. Get monthly Unlimited Starter for $10/mo or Premium for $20/mo for the first 6 months."

So, in plain terms:

- **Port-in only? Yes.** You must transfer an existing number from another carrier. A new number does not qualify.
- **New lines only?** The plan card labels it a "New line offer," and the number being ported must come from outside US Mobile. The promo attaches to the ported-in line.
- **Second line on an existing account?** Not addressed in so many words on the public pages read. The terms are written per ported-in line, with a cap of 5 per household, which suggests multiple ported lines in one household can each qualify — but whether an *existing* US Mobile customer porting an additional line gets the promo is **unsure** from official public text (see Unsure about).
- **VoIP and number-broker numbers are excluded** — so a Google Voice or other VoIP number cannot be the number you port in for this promo.
- **End date: October 20, 2026.** No end *time* or time zone is stated on the official pages read — treat Oct 20 as the last day and don't cut it fine.
- Code **FBF10** is entered at checkout.

## 2. Port-in steps, in order (from US Mobile's official help docs)

Source: "Activating or transferring your number," US Mobile help docs — https://www.usmobile.com/help/docs/getting-started/activate-or-transfer-number

1. **Buy the plan first** (with code FBF10), then start activation from your Lines dashboard in the app. Activation is three steps: choose a network, choose a SIM type, then transfer your number.
2. **Before you submit anything, get two things from your current carrier:** (a) your **account number** — "usually printed on your bill"; (b) your **transfer PIN** — how you get one differs by carrier, and US Mobile links a carrier-by-carrier porting guide from the same help page. **Keep your old line active and do not cancel it first** — US Mobile's own transfer guides warn the old account "must remain active during the porting process. Don't cancel it prematurely, or you might lose your number."
3. **Choose your network** — Dark Star, Warp, or Light Speed (you can switch networks later).
4. **Choose eSIM or physical SIM** — US Mobile recommends eSIM for compatible devices.
5. **Submit the transfer** with your account number and transfer PIN. The details "must match your current carrier's records exactly — including formatting. Even a minor mismatch (a different address abbreviation, an incorrect PIN digit) will cause the transfer to fail."
6. **Install the SIM/eSIM** (install link or QR code from your Line page or confirmation email; or swap in the physical SIM).
7. **Wait for completion and test.** US Mobile: "Most number transfers complete **within ten minutes** — however, some transfers can take **up to 48 hours** depending on when your previous carrier releases your number." Do the transfer during regular business hours for the smoothest run, since the old carrier releases the number manually. When it completes you get a confirmation email; test a call, a text, and cellular data.

**What happens to the old line:** you keep service on it until the port completes (that's why it must stay active). US Mobile's public pages read for this task do not state in so many words that the old line then cancels automatically — see Unsure about.

**What can go wrong:**
- **Detail mismatch** — the big one. A failed transfer gets you an email and "can add several days of delay"; you fix it by resubmitting the account number/PIN from your Line page (Update → Submit).
- **Slow release by the old carrier** — up to 48 hours; worse outside business hours.
- **VoIP numbers** — transfers of Google Voice/VoIP numbers "require additional steps and aren't always possible" per the help doc — and for this promo they are excluded outright anyway.

## 3. True first-month cost

- **Plan: $10 for the first month**, and the plans page states **"Taxes & fees included"** for these plans — so $10 is the plan total, not $10 plus tax. (US Mobile's general FAQ notes some *other* plans carry a $2 service fee plus $1–$5 recovery fees, but the Unlimited plans shown on the plans page are the taxes-and-fees-included kind.)
- **eSIM: no charge** is stated anywhere on the official pages — activation by eSIM is presented as the default, free route.
- **Physical SIM:** the official Starter Kit (Dark Star + Warp SIMs, ejector, guide) is **$6.99** in US Mobile's shop — https://www.usmobile.com/shop/product/starter-kit — and per the terms, SIM and shipping fees are non-refundable. Shipping cost for the kit is not stated on the product page read.
- **So first month is $10 all-in with eSIM, or $16.99 plus any shipping with a physical kit.**
- **Duration and after:** the $10 price covers the **first 6 months** (US Mobile bills in 30-day cycles), then the line **renews at $25/month** — the standard monthly Unlimited Starter price — unless you change or cancel. Six months at $10, then $25, is the honest math: $60 for the promo half-year vs $150 at full price.

## 4. Catches

- **AutoPay is effectively mandatory.** US Mobile's terms (§4.2–4.3, https://www.usmobile.com/terms): lines "must keep Autopay enabled with a valid credit card on file," and "if you cancel automatic billing, you may lose certain promotions or discounts." So the promo assumes a card on file auto-charging every 30 days.
- **Data is "unlimited," with soft edges.** Per the plans-page FAQ: on **Warp and Light Speed**, "you may experience slower speeds after **70 GB** of monthly usage" on Unlimited Starter; on **Dark Star**, Starter is marketed as unlimited high-speed data. The plan card lists **20 GB of hotspot on Dark Star**; a hotspot allowance for the other two networks is not stated on the pages read.
- **Network is a choice you make at activation** — Dark Star, Warp, or Light Speed (US Mobile runs on all three major US networks). Network transfers between them are free for the first two (ever) and $2 after, unless the line has the free-transfer feature (plans-page FAQ).
- **No contract, but prepaid rules apply.** No contract and no minimum term; you pay each 30-day cycle up front. But prepaid amounts are generally non-refundable (terms §5.3), SIM/Starter Kit purchases are non-refundable (§5.4), and **porting out mid-cycle forfeits the remaining days** of the cycle (§4.2). Plans must be activated within 90 days of purchase (§3).
- **Household cap:** limit 5 promo lines per household.
- **The promo is a discount on the standard plan, not a special plan** — after month 6 you are simply on ordinary $25/month Unlimited Starter, so the "deal" is $90 saved over six months, not a permanent price.

Unsure from official public text.
