## (c) AT&T Prepaid port-out

**Correction first:** AT&T's own FAQ for the Number Transfer PIN article states: **"AT&T Prepaid accounts don't need a Number Transfer PIN. All current requirements will stay the same for AT&T Prepaid number transfer requests."** Source (FAQ section of the article): https://services.att.com/es-us/kmservices/v2/contents/KM1447526?app-id=idpui

So for **AT&T Prepaid** specifically:

1. Keep the line active — do not cancel/disconnect before the new carrier completes the transfer (same article, general rule).
2. What the new carrier needs instead of a transfer PIN: your **AT&T Prepaid account number + your account PIN/passcode** ("current requirements stay the same").
3. **Account number:** AT&T's Prepaid page says: "To get your AT&T PREPAID account number, call us" — AT&T lists its Prepaid support number on that page (611 from the AT&T phone also reaches Prepaid support). There is no self-serve web display of the Prepaid account number on the official pages checked. Source: https://www.att.com/support/article/wireless/KM1045139
4. **Port-out / PIN fee: none.** The same article's FAQ: "Is there a charge for Number Transfer PIN or for transferring my number? No." Source: https://services.att.com/es-us/kmservices/v2/contents/KM1447526?app-id=idpui
5. **Balance:** "AT&T PREPAID cards and other account payments are nontransferable and nonrefundable"; unused account balance is forfeited at expiration, and the plan charge is not refundable — porting out does not get the balance back. Baseline "prepaid balance is not refunded on port" = **VERIFIED in substance**. Sources: https://www.att.com/legal/terms.consumerServiceAgreement.html?LinkID=CSA and https://www.att.com/legal/terms.prepaidAutoPayTerms.html

For completeness — the baseline steps describe **postpaid AT&T Wireless** (not Prepaid), and for that product they are accurate:

- Online: sign in → account profile → **Settings** → scroll to **Transfer phone number** → **Request new PIN** (PIN displays on screen). App: profile icon → Settings → Manage or remove account → Settings → Transfer phone number → Request new PIN. Or dial **\*PORT** from the AT&T device (needs account passcode; PIN sent by text). Primary-access users only.
- **PIN expires 4 days** after request (14 days for business). Same PIN covers all numbers on the account.
- **Wireless Account Lock ON blocks the transfer** — turn it off first.
- Source for all of the above: https://www.att.com/support/smallbusiness/article/smb-wireless/KM1447526/
- Whether Wireless Account Lock applies to a Prepaid line the same way: the page does not say — **UNKNOWN**.
