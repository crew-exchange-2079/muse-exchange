## Price bands

- **Free** — the entire training stack (trainers, notebooks, GUIs, Colab/Kaggle free tiers). Nobody can charge for the mechanics; free itch.io trainers (Reanimation Labs, NMKD) now package the same mechanics one click away.
- **Under $10** — per-run compute and the cheapest packaged teaching: Civitai ~$0.50/run, LoRA AI $2/run, finetuna $5/job, the $7 masterclass and the $9 spreadsheet flow. This band is where a first-time buyer experiments.
- **$10–$30** — the proven LLM-kit band: Local AI Master's $19 kit (the only true LLM fine-tuning kit found for sale), the $24 eBook and the $24 Leanpub book. What justifies it over the sub-$10 band: a complete end-to-end pipeline with verified configs, sample data, eval and export — not a single trick.
- **$30 and up** — bundles, done-for-you jobs and upkeep: the $99 all-kits bundle, finetuna's $39 large job, LoRA AI Pro $29.90/mo, ThinkDiffusion TD-PRO $19.99–$29.99/mo, SECourses' membership (price hidden). Above $30, buyers are paying for upkeep, compute, or a human — not files.

## Gaps no listing covers well

1. **No social proof in the LLM segment.** Across this scan, not one listing showed a review count, rating or sales number. (In the October scan the only listing with any visible reviews was a $40 ComfyUI/RunPod pack with 6 reviews; it was not re-verified today.) A kit that publishes real user results stands out immediately.
2. **The outcome layer is unsold.** Kits sell knobs — configs, presets, installers. Nobody sells "your messy data in, a plain-English verdict on whether the result is any good out": dataset preparation from real-world files, and an eval scorecard that says pass/fail. finetuna comes closest with its base-vs-tuned report, but it is a done-for-you service, not a kit.
3. **No priced middle and no local-hardware guarantee.** Nothing sells "kit + help getting your first run working," and priced kits assume cloud or an unspecified machine. A kit built and benchmarked for a local 24 GB card (RTX 3090/4090), Windows included, with measured VRAM and wall-clock per recipe, has no direct competitor. For scale: done-for-you fine-tuning services start far above this band (the October scan found a $450 median starting tier on Upwork).
