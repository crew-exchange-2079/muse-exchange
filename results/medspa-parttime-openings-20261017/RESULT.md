## (b) WV licensing board, and moving an out-of-state license into WV

**Board: West Virginia Board of Barbers and Cosmetologists (WVBBC)** — https://www.wvbbc.com (also listed in the official state agency directory at wv.gov/agencies). A license is mandatory to work as an aesthetician/esthetician in WV. The board issues a single-level **"Aesthetician"** license (no tiers stated), requiring a minimum of **600 clock hours** of training (board rule Title 3 §3-1-9).

**Reciprocity — the steps** (from the board's official transfer page, FAQ and reciprocity application, form rev. July 2025):

1. **Get verifications sent first.** Ask your current state board to send your license certification and training verification directly to the WVBBC (some states take 4–6 weeks). If you trained in a different state from the one that licensed you, that state's board must also send training verification. Verifications must arrive **before** you submit the application; school transcripts alone are not accepted as verification.
2. **Submit the Application for Registration by Reciprocity** with: form pages 1–3; notarized school transcript; name-change proof if applicable; copy of Social Security card; copy of photo ID; a recent photo; a completed Certificate of Health (exam by a physician, PA or NP — the application must be submitted within 1 year of the exam); high-school diploma, GED or ATB; and a copy of your out-of-state license.
3. **Fee: $100** by check or money order (the figure is stated in the application form's checklist, not on the webpage).
4. **Mail** the application to WVBBC – Reciprocity, P.O. Box 40235, Charleston, WV 25364. (Verification mailing/email addresses are on the board's transfer page.) West Virginia does **not** require you to put your current license on inactive status. Legal basis: W. Va. Code §30-1-27.

Official pages: https://www.wvbbc.com/License-Services/Transfer-License-to-WV · https://www.wvbbc.com/License-Services/FAQ
