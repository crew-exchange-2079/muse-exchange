# Small-business IT pain points & what IT help costs — north-central WV

*One-page brief for planning a small IT-help service for very small businesses (1–20 staff) in Harrison, Marion, Monongalia, Lewis and Upshur counties. Public sources only; prices retrieved 2026-10-06.*

## 1. Ten typical pain points, ranked by how often they come up

1. **Everyday glitches eat staff time** — 49% of employees estimate they lose 1–5 hours a week to IT issues (2023, [Unisys/HFS Research](https://www.unisys.com/news-release/hfs-partnership-research-surviving-to-thriving-in-hybrid-work/), vendor research).
2. **No IT person on staff** — 49% of SME IT professionals say they lack the resources and staffing to secure their organisation (2024, [JumpCloud SME IT Trends](https://jumpcloud.com/blog/sme-it-report-q3-2024), vendor survey).
3. **Phishing and email scams** — phishing was the initial access vector in 16% of breaches; the human element figured in 62% (2026, [Verizon DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T8a5/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf)).
4. **Weak passwords, missing MFA** — credential abuse played a part in 39% of breaches at some point in the attack (2026, [Verizon DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T8a5/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf)).
5. **Ransomware** — present in 88% of breaches at SMB-sized organisations (2025, [Verizon DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/about/news/2025-data-breach-investigations-report)).
6. **Unpatched, outdated software** — exploiting known vulnerabilities was the initial vector in 20% of breaches, up 34% year on year (2025, [Verizon DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/about/news/2025-data-breach-investigations-report)).
7. **Weak backups, slow recovery** — 8% of organisations don't back up public-cloud data at all; 40% would need days or weeks to recover it (2025, [Kaseya backup report](https://www.kaseya.com/press-release/kaseya-report-outlines-top-trends-in-backup-and-recovery-in-2025/), vendor survey).
8. **Supporting home/remote workers** — 42% of SME IT admins named the cost of remote-work solutions a top IT challenge (2024, [JumpCloud Q1 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/02/14/2828887/0/en/JumpCloud-s-Q1-2024-SME-IT-Trends-Report-Reveals-AI-Optimism-Tempered-by-Security-Concerns.html), vendor survey).
9. **Ageing hardware, surprise replacements** — 23% of businesses named legacy systems a challenge hampering growth (2024, [Kaseya Future of IT Survey](https://www.kaseya.com/download/20886/?version_id=20889), vendor survey).
10. **Downtime costs real money** — average $126,000 including lost revenue for SMBs that had a cybersecurity incident (2022, [Datto SMB report](https://www.datto.com/pt-BR/download/datto-smb-cybersecurity-for-msps-report/?tmstv=1788416773), vendor survey — oldest figure used, see Unsure).

Just outside the top 10: cyber-insurance/compliance pressure — a third (33%) of attacked SMEs were hit with a substantial fine (2025, [Hiscox Cyber Readiness Report](https://www.hiscoxgroup.com/hiscox-cyber-readiness-report-2025), insurer survey).

## 3. Three takeaways for pricing a small new service

1. **Publishing your prices is itself the local edge.** Nobody in north-central WV posts a managed price; the few visible local numbers are flat fees ($40 diagnostic, $60 tune-up, $95 virus removal). Clear flat prices in that range meet the market where it visibly is.
2. **Per-user pricing scares tiny offices.** Regional plans at $100–$250 per user/month mean $1,000+/month for a 10-person office. One flat office price is simpler to understand and easier to say yes to.
3. **Lead with the daily pain, bundle the safety basics.** What owners feel weekly is glitches and "nobody here does IT" — sell fast, friendly help first. Put MFA setup, patching and tested backups inside the base plan rather than as extras; the breach numbers above (88% ransomware in SMB breaches, credential abuse in 39%) are the honest reason they belong there.

## How I checked it

Every statistic and price was read on its source page, not taken from a search snippet. Second-page confirmations: Verizon's 2025 DBIR release page independently carries the 88% SMB ransomware figure, the 20% vulnerability-exploitation figure and the +34% surge; I re-opened Tekswift's repair page (virus removal "start at $95", screen repair "starts at $175") and E-N Computers' pricing page ($125 / $75 / $37 per user/month) directly. Vendor and insurer surveys are labelled as such next to each figure, and directory/third-party price claims (e.g. a Clutch hourly range for one local firm) were excluded — only prices on a shop's own public page, or clearly flagged directory listings, are shown.

## Unsure about

- No survey found samples only 1–20-staff businesses: the DBIR figures cover breaches of all sizes (the 88% is the SMB cut), and JumpCloud/Kaseya/Datto surveyed SMEs broadly (Datto: 10–300 staff). Read the percentages as "small-business neighbourhood", not exact for a five-person office.
- The downtime figure is the oldest used (2022 survey data) and covers downtime after a cyber incident, not all downtime; no verified 2023–2026 micro-business downtime cost was found.
- "Not published" means no price appeared on the pages opened on 2026-10-06; shops may quote prices elsewhere. Global Science & Technology's site only rendered a fragment, and some local shops' pages failed to load during checking, so the local table is not exhaustive. No standalone Weston IT shop with a working website could be verified.
- JNM PC Experts' $60 tune-up appears on a directory listing rather than the shop's own site, so it is weaker evidence than the other published prices.
