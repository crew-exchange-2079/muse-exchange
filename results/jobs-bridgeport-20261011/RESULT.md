## Summary

Profile used for fit ranking: licensed esthetician, about 1.5 years of college (no degree), clean background, likes computers, open to part-time or full-time, any field. Pay priority: $17+/hr or $35k+/yr.

30 openings are tabled below, sorted by fit for that profile, not by pay alone. The strongest fits are direct-license esthetician roles in Fairmont, Clarksburg, and Morgantown, dermatology front-office / clinical-support roles that value skin knowledge plus computer / EHR work, and hospital patient-registration roles that require only a high-school diploma plus a background check. ## Patterns — 5 bullets

- **Who hires most for this profile:** WVU Medicine / United Hospital Center and Mon Health together account for the largest cluster — patient registration, patient access and clinic front-office roles across Bridgeport, White Hall/Fairmont and Morgantown — followed by Tuscan Sun Spa & Salon, which was simultaneously advertising esthetician, laser technician, med-spa sales, front-desk and other roles across Fairmont, Clarksburg and Morgantown, and by DOCS Dermatology in Bridgeport/Morgantown. Citynet in Bridgeport is the standout non-healthcare local employer for computer-oriented work.
- **Typical pay, honestly:** direct esthetician work is the clearest route to the target — $20/hr starting at Massage Envy Morgantown, and $20+/hr total at European Wax Center, though the wax role's base is only $10/hr and the rest is tips/commission. Hospital registration pay is usually *not listed*, and employee-reported medians for Mon Health registration roles (roughly $14.62–$17.67/hr) suggest many of those roles sit at or below the $17 line until confirmed. Local admin/intake roles cluster at $16–$20.67/hr, and the cleanest at-or-above-target pay with no degree was in remote customer service / patient scheduling ($17.75–$20/hr).
- **What blocks most roles:** a bachelor's degree is the single biggest blocker — it closes most WV state professional roles, university roles, and essentially all FBI CJIS and NASA IV&V contractor engineering roles, which additionally expect specialised experience and a Top Secret / TS-SCI clearance path. The second blocker is a *different* licence or certification than esthetics: medical-assistant duties (phlebotomy, injections, suture removal), laser certification, and pharmacy experience each gate otherwise-adjacent roles. The third is specific experience — 1 year of medical-office / Epic-EMR patient-access work, or 3 years of front-desk experience — which is why the no-experience remote appointment-setting role and the high-school-only hospital registrar roles rank as practical entry points.
- **Where this profile's strengths actually convert:** a clean background is explicitly useful — hospital registrar listings name a state/federal background check as a core requirement, and VA, bank, pharmacy and federal-adjacent roles all screen. "Likes computers" converts best in patient registration / scheduling, Citynet technical support, and remote CRM/EHR customer-service roles, all of which are computer-and-phone jobs that do not require a degree. The esthetician licence itself converts fully only in spa/med-spa/dermatology settings — elsewhere it reads as customer-service and skin-care experience, not as a clinical credential.
- **Pay and date transparency is the local norm to work around:** a majority of local listings in this scan listed neither pay nor a visible posted date, and board "posted" times often reflect aggregator crawl times rather than the employer's original posting date. Any shortlist from this table should be re-verified on the employer's own site for current status and pay before time is spent on an application — especially the roles marked "not listed" for pay, and the two remote healthcare roles where state-residency eligibility needs confirming.
