### 1. Ona for Open Source — up to $200/month in credits for open-source maintainers (NEW)

- **Provider:** Ona (ona.com — mission control for software engineering agents; sandboxed dev environments, VS Code in the browser, GitHub/GitLab integration)
- **What you get:** Up to $200 per month in Ona credits. The amount granted depends on the degree of the applicant's open-source contributions. Credits are for using Ona to accelerate open-source work (managing AI-generated PRs, melting backlogs, automated review/test/build checks).
- **Eligibility (individual OK?) — CONDITION, stated prominently:** You must be a maintainer or core contributor of at least one active, well-established open-source project. This is a genuine-contribution gate, not a company, incorporation, or VC gate — a solo developer who maintains or core-contributes to an active OSS project plausibly qualifies; a developer with no OSS footprint does not. No company, funding, or referral requirement is stated.
- **Card required?:** NOT FOUND — Ona's program page does not state whether a payment method is needed.
- **Region limits:** NOT FOUND.
- **How to claim:** Apply on Ona's own program page, https://ona.com/open-source — fill out the application form with your GitHub profile and the repositories you maintain or contribute to. Ona reviews the application and verifies eligibility; most applications are processed within a few days. No promo code is mentioned.
- **Expiry:** No end date stated on the provider page. Monthly credits (amount re-granted per month, not a one-time grant).
- **Why this counts as new:** no prior series file mentions Ona (dedup grep empty apart from false positives on the substring "ona" inside words like "optional"); the freetokens listing was published today, 2026-11-20, and the provider page confirms the terms.
- **Sources:** Ona's own program page, https://ona.com/open-source (read 2026-11-20; up to $200/month, maintainer/core-contributor eligibility, GitHub-profile application, processed within days, no end date stated); tracker listing https://freetokens.custats.info/offers/ona-for-open-source.html (published 2026-11-20).
- **Worth it for a homelab AI crew?** Yes, if the crew maintains an active open-source project — $200/month of coding-agent compute is the largest recurring grant in this series for an individual, and it fits the crew's actual OSS maintenance work (public repos, agent-driven PR review). Not claimable on a homelab alone; the OSS footprint is the whole gate.

### 2. Zoom AI Launchpad — up to $150,000 in Zoom AI Services credits for AI-first startups (NEW)

- **What you get:** Grant tiers: Seed $5,000 / Growth $50,000 / Scale $150,000 in platform credits, plus priority/early API access, technical support (community to dedicated engineer by tier), founder network, co-marketing, and workshops by tier. Credits valid 12 months from issuance; community/support benefits for the 12-month program period.
- **Eligibility (individual OK?) — CONDITION, stated prominently:** This is a startup grant, not a signup credit. Zoom's page says it is looking for "ambitious startups and emerging companies" that are early- to growth-stage, have "a small to mid-sized product and engineering team", are building a product or service with AI at its core, and have "a working prototype, production product, or clear technical roadmap". Funding stage is explicitly NOT a strict requirement ("we evaluate each application based on the product, team, technical fit, and potential to build and scale"), and prior funding does not disqualify. No incorporation requirement and no VC/accelerator referral is stated on the page. A solo founder is therefore not formally excluded, but the "team" language and the product/traction evaluation make a one-person homelab crew a weak applicant unless it has a genuine AI-first product to point at.

- **Region limits:** Worldwide — the FAQ says "We welcome applications from eligible startups worldwide, subject to applicable laws, regulations, and program eligibility requirements."
- **How to claim:** Apply on Zoom's own program page, https://zoom.ai/ai-launchpad — short application form (under 10 minutes per Zoom: team, product, how you plan to use AI), then review and a 20-minute interview for shortlisted founders. Applications open now, reviewed on a rolling basis, limited spots. Announced Nov 10, 2026.
- **Expiry:** No application deadline stated; rolling review, limited number accepted per cohort. Credits valid 12 months from issuance once granted.
- **Why this counts as new:** no prior series file mentions Zoom (dedup grep empty); announced Nov 10, 2026 via GlobeNewswire and live on Zoom's own page today; the series' targeted searches had not surfaced it before today.
- **Sources:** Zoom's own program page, https://zoom.ai/ai-launchpad (read 2026-11-20; tiers $5,000/$50,000/$150,000, 12-month validity, rolling review, worldwide, funding stage not strict); announcement https://idc.chase.com/newspublic/story?article=389b21e26d65b5f2&controller=newspublic&action=search&module=default (GlobeNewswire, Nov 10, 2026).
