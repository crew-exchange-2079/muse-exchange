## Scam / trap flags

- **$1 "prices" are auction placeholders, not sale prices:** [321 Despard St, Clarksburg](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/321-Despard-St-Clarksburg-WV-26301/22523278_zpid/) (5bd/4ba, 4,320 sq ft, auction), 2 Stadium Dr, Clarksburg ($1 auction on Zillow/Homes.com — the property is the ~16-acre Hite Field, not a normal house sale), and [4 Jo Harry Dr, Fairmont](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/4-Jo-Harry-Dr-Fairmont-WV-26554/22724638_zpid/) ($1 auction placeholder, 22 days on Zillow). The listings themselves state the $1 is neither the reserve nor the list price.
- **Assessed-value auctions in Bridgeport:** 224 Broadway Ave (row 23, $110,700 = assessed value only) and [409 Hinkle Lake Rd](https://www.homes.com/property/409-hinkle-lake-rd-bridgeport-wv/rfggbjn01gr33/) (~$118,700, same warning; Zillow: https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/409-Hinkle-Lake-Rd-Bridgeport-WV-26330/465210241_zpid/), plus [513 Glen Ave](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/513-Glen-Ave-Bridgeport-WV-26330/22543290_zpid/) (auction, no price shown, 2 days on Zillow).
- **Another auction:** [1742 S 28th St, Clarksburg](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/1742-S-28th-St-Clarksburg-WV-26301/22532265_zpid/) — $15,000 auction, 796 sq ft, 291 days on Zillow.
- **Rentals wearing sale prices:** Marketplace housing searches for all three towns are full of $750–$1,500 listings — those are monthly rents, not purchase prices. Any "house for $1,050" in Clarksburg or "$1,250" in Fairmont in the feed is a rental.
- **Too-cheap "houses" that are not houses on land:** the $5,000 Clarksburg single-wide (https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/4517946021827579, generic description, no land mentioned) and row 4's $15,000 Stonewood mobile home (land not stated).
- **Not actually for sale:** a $55,000 Clarksburg post titled "Land or house" (https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/1670373384472730) is a buyer ISO post looking to purchase 1–5 acres in Harrison County. A $45,000 Haywood post titled as a 3-bed house (https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/1394325089029370) has a description that says only "Land for sale." Land-only posts also appear at house-adjacent prices (e.g., a $6,000 two-lot Fairmont land listing and $4,000–$6,000 Clarksburg lots with land-contract offers).
- **Group-level bait:** national Facebook groups that surface in housing searches ("Houses for rent by private owners," ~124K members; "House For Rent," ~44K members) run Section 8 / HUD-foreclosure / rent-to-own funnels that push members to off-platform links — the ecosystem where deposit-by-gift-card and fake-landlord scams live. Avoid; the legitimate local/regional groups are listed below. No gift-card language and no rent-to-own language was found in the actual local listings checked on Oct 11.
- **Stale ultra-cheap MLS:** rows 2 and 7 at 397 days on market, row 5 at 177 days — at these prices and durations, verify the property is really available and the title is clean before trusting the listing.

## How I checked it

- Facebook Marketplace searched Oct 11 via the Facebook CLI with location-centred queries (Clarksburg 39.2806,-80.3445; Fairmont 39.4851,-80.1426; Bridgeport 39.2965,-80.2512; 15–20 mi radius; max price $175,000; queries: house, home for sale, property for sale, fixer upper, for sale by owner, house as is). 57 unique listings found; rentals, vehicles, trailers and out-of-area results filtered out. Full listing details (description, beds/baths, sq ft, creation date) were then pulled for every in-scope sale candidate; days listed = Oct 11 minus the listing creation date. Look-only throughout.
- MLS comparison from two live browser passes on Oct 11 covering Zillow, Redfin and Homes.com with town + max $175,000 + house filters, spot-checking detail pages. Realtor.com was attempted in both passes and rendered only a blank/block page both times, so no Realtor.com data is claimed.
- Groups: one group (Homes & Land of West Virginia) verified live via the Facebook CLI on Oct 11 (name, public status, 19,000 members, rules). Facebook's own group search does not surface most public groups to a non-member account, so member/activity figures for the other four come from a public Facebook-group directory (mygroupfinder.com West Virginia list) and are labelled as directory figures.
- Cross-checks: Marketplace addresses (401 Elm St, 60 Craig Run Rd, 315 E Main St) matched against the MLS sample; same-price/same-date comparison for the unaddressed $89,900 Clarksburg garage house vs 2861 Wilsonburg Rd; Spring Ave price matched against the crew's earlier price ladder and the condemnation finding already documented in this repo.
- Privacy scan before commit: no private names, no phone numbers (one Marketplace seller's phone number appears in their own listing text; it is noted as existing but not reproduced), no addresses beyond what the public listings themselves publish.
