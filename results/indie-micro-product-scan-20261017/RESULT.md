## Patterns

1. **Prices sit at two poles, with an empty middle.** The set is either very cheap one-off files ($3–$5 patterns and printables, rows 1–3) that win on volume, or $25–$100 tools (rows 6–11) that win on one sharp job. Almost nothing lives at $10–$15/month — the nearest is P7M.io's €15.99/year (row 8).
2. **Search does the selling; nobody in the set buys ads.** Etsy search (rows 1–3), the Cults3D marketplace (row 4) and Google SEO (row 8) supply the buyers. The content-led exceptions — Medium articles (row 6), SEO guides (row 7), tutorials (row 5) — are still search-shaped, and ad spend is $0 wherever it's mentioned.
3. **At the low end, upload cadence beats catalogue size.** With identical patterns in three test stores, one new pattern a week earned ~18× the set-and-forget store (row 3). Marketplace "freshness" is a ranking input, not a nicety.
4. **First sales are slow unless a marketplace hands you buyers.** Row 7 took ~5–6 months to break a flat line despite steady content work; the marketplace rows (1–4) don't even report a first-sale struggle, because demand was already sitting in the marketplace's search box.
5. **Verified numbers are smaller and spikier than self-reported ones.** The payment-verified rows (8–11) show $200–$485/month with one-time-download spikes on top of a small base (row 8: $485 MRR vs $4.7k in 30 days). The self-reported rows run higher and smoother — read them with a discount.

## Three copy-and-improve ideas (each buildable in under two weeks)

1. **A mandated-format helper** (from row 8, P7M.io). Find one file or document format that a specific trade or public is *forced* to receive and that no common OS or app opens well, and ship a private, in-browser reader/verifier with a ~$4 one-off / ~$16-a-year unlock. Client-side only keeps it weekend-scale, and SEO on the exact "[format] opener" searches is the whole distribution channel — row 8 ranks #3 on Google with zero paid acquisition.
2. **One template, twenty niches** (from rows 1 and 3). Take one strong printable design and clone it across keyword-researched niches on Etsy at ~$5, then commit to the weekly-upload cadence that made row 3's test store outperform its set-and-forget twin ~18-to-1. Row 1 shows the per-item design cost can be 15–20 minutes once the base template exists.
3. **A one-platform, plain-English reporter** (from row 7, 1ClickReport). Pull ONE platform's numbers (a shop's Google Business Profile, a seller's Etsy stats) into a single plain-language report page at ~$25/month, and distribute it with short guides answering the exact questions those owners already google. Scoping to a single data source is what keeps it inside two weeks; row 7 reached ~$750/month on content alone.

## How I checked it

Public pages only, all read 2026-10-17. Prices come from each product's own page or the maker's own post (labelled where no storefront exists). Revenue comes only from (a) a first-person post or interview by the maker (labelled self-reported), (b) Indie Hackers/Starter-Story-type interviews where the maker speaks to their own numbers, or (c) TrustMRR listings, which are **payment-verified** — TrustMRR reads revenue from the maker's payment provider through a read-only API key rather than taking the maker's word. SEO listicles with no named product and no first-party numbers were ignored. Two preferred categories were searched and came up empty at this evidence bar — no homelab/self-hosting tool and no Notion/template pack with credible, named, in-band revenue (only sellers far above $10K/month or anonymous strategy posts) — so they were dropped, not padded. Several near-misses were checked and dropped for being out of band, stale, or weak-evidence (e.g., a calendar-sync tool whose maker now reports ~$1,500 MRR; a launch spike with no ongoing rate).

## Unsure about

- **Aquireef3D (row 4):** figures are cumulative per model with no stated sales period, so no monthly rate is claimed — the row qualifies on totals that plainly imply a modest monthly pace, the weakest evidence in the table. Its live prices could not be captured because Cults3D blocks text fetching (403), as does Etsy; rows 1–4 therefore rest on first-person posts rather than storefront reads.
- **Row 1's shop is deliberately unnamed** by its maker, so there is no public product URL to check the $5 price against a live listing.
- **Better Sheets (row 5):** the $1,000-day figure is a 2020 spike snapshot from the period when the shop sat in this band; it has since grown beyond it, and no monthly baseline for that period is published.
- **Carry (row 9):** the $59 price comes from the maker's launch post, not a storefront page (none surfaced); its traffic channel, launch date and build effort are not publicly evidenced.
- **VoiceBrief (row 11):** price conflict — the product's own page shows Pro at $99.99/year while its TrustMRR card describes $19.99/month; the own-page figure is used and the conflict is flagged here.
- Launch → first-sale timing and build effort are simply not published for most rows; the table says "Not stated" instead of estimating them.
