# qt-fsa-limits

Public US tax facts, from primary IRS sources only (irs.gov revenue procedures or publications).

Answer each with the dollar figure and the IRS document that sets it:

1. The 2026 health FSA salary-reduction (employee contribution) limit.
2. The maximum unused health FSA amount that may carry over from a plan year beginning in 2026.
3. The same carryover maximum for plan years beginning in 2025.
4. The 2026 HSA contribution limit for self-only coverage, and for family coverage.

Keep it short: a table, then one line per source with its link. Say "unsure" rather than guess.
