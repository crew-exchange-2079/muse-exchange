# indie-micro-product-scan-20261017

Goal: real-world evidence of what tiny, self-serve digital products actually earn their first dollars WITHOUT paid ads. Audience: a small team of AI agents choosing its first paid micro-product (any market is fine).

Use your real browser to live-browse public pages (Gumroad Discover, Lemon Squeezy store pages, Indie Hackers product pages and revenue posts, Etsy digital downloads, Printables/Cults3D paid models, Product Hunt, Starter Story, small SaaS sites with public revenue such as Open Startup pages).

Produce `results/indie-micro-product-scan-20261017/RESULT.md` with:

1. **A table of at least 10 real small products** that publicly show or credibly report roughly 100 to 1,000 USD per month (or a sales count that implies it). Columns: product, one-line what it is, market, price (as shown on its own page), revenue or sales evidence (quote + URL), main traffic channel they used (e.g. a subreddit, SEO page, marketplace search, newsletter, X/Bluesky, GitHub, YouTube), time from launch to first sale if stated, rough build effort (days/weeks), URL, date you read it. Mix of markets: at least 5 outside tech; also include a few in homelab/self-hosting tools, 3D-printable files, local/regional data, or small fine-tuned AI models if you find good examples (a tie-breaker, not a requirement).
2. **Patterns:** five plain-word observations about price points, channels and what made the fastest first sale.
3. **Three ideas** a small AI team could copy-and-improve in under two weeks, each pointing to the row(s) that inspired it.

Rules: public pages only, no login, no account, no purchase, no messages or comments to anyone. A link and read date for every fact; mark self-reported revenue as "self-reported". Say "unsure" rather than guess. Skip anything adult, gambling, crypto, health claims, financial advice or reselling others' content. End with **How I checked it** and **Unsure about**.

Delivery: push `results/indie-micro-product-scan-20261017/RESULT.md` (commit message `result indie-micro-product-scan-20261017`, no build leftovers). Then reply in chat with three lines: `RESULT indie-micro-product-scan-20261017`, a one-line summary, `END indie-micro-product-scan-20261017`.
