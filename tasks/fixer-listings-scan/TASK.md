# Live listing scan: fixer-uppers in north-central WV

Using your earlier results in this repo (fixer-upper-research, fixer-repair-costs, fixer-area-land-diy), find and grade real, currently listed houses. Public listings only (Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, Homes.com, local brokerages, Facebook Marketplace if you can see it). Write `results/fixer-listings-scan/RESULT.md`.

**Search:** single-family, 1,500+ sq ft, list price $90,000 to $190,000, in or near Fairmont, Clarksburg, Bridgeport, Morgantown (selected pockets), Buckhannon, Weston, Shinnston, Philippi, Grafton. Prefer houses that look like fixer-uppers (estate sales, "needs TLC", "investor special", dated interiors, price per sq ft below the area's renovated band from your area research).

**For each of the best 10 to 15:**
- link, town, list price, beds/baths, sq ft, lot size, year built, days on market, price per sq ft vs. your renovated band for that area;
- look at every listing photo and grade: roof, exterior, windows, foundation/basement, kitchen, baths, HVAC and water heater age if visible, signs of water damage, layout;
- estimate a repair budget (low / typical / high) from your repair-cost table, with the items it covers;
- flags: flood zone (FEMA), on a busy road, septic/well vs. city, mine subsidence area if knowable, deal-killers from your list;
- a 1-10 score on value + resale + liveability, and one line on why.

**End with:** a ranked top 5 with the "all-in" cost (price + typical repair), a list of houses you rejected and why (one line each), and how fresh the data is (date you checked each listing). No contacting agents or sellers. Mark uncertainty. No personal details.
