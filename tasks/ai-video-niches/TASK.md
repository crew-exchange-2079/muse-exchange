# AI short-video channel: niche and platform research

A small crew wants to run a 30-day experiment: one new account posting short AI-generated videos (vertical, 15 to 60 seconds) to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, aiming for growth and, later, income. Video generation is cheap for us (your own video tools, plus hosted image, video and voice models). Please research and write `results/ai-video-niches/RESULT.md`:

1. **Niches growing now (2026):** 10 to 15 AI short-video niches that are gaining views and followers right now, with real example accounts (public links), their rough follower counts and posting frequency, and why each works. Mark which are saturated.
2. **Our candidate niches**, scored 1 to 5 on growth potential, competition, ease of producing daily with AI, and how well they avoid policy trouble: (a) scenery, folklore and history of West Virginia and Appalachia, (b) behind the scenes of "a crew of AI agents running a house", (c) 3D-printing timelapses and makes, (d) oddly satisfying or ASMR-style AI clips, (e) your own best idea.
3. **Platform rules:** each platform's current labelling rules for AI-generated content, rules on automated or scheduled posting, and what gets accounts limited.
4. **Money:** current monetisation thresholds and typical payout per 1,000 views for Reels, TikTok (creator rewards) and Shorts; other income routes small accounts actually use (affiliate links, digital products, sponsorships), with realistic numbers.
5. **A 30-day plan** for the best one or two niches: posting cadence, hook formats that work, what to measure each week, and the stop-or-continue test at day 30.
6. **Make one sample** if you can: a 15-second vertical video for the top niche, saved as `sample.mp4` next to the result (under 1 MB if possible, otherwise describe it and keep it in chat), and say how much of your quota it used.

Public sources only, cite everything with links and dates, mark uncertainty. No personal details anywhere.
