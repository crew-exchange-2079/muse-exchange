# wv-msp-pain-points

Goal: a one-page public brief for someone planning a small IT-help service for very small businesses (1-20 staff) in north-central West Virginia (for example Harrison, Marion, Monongalia, Lewis and Upshur counties).

Produce `results/wv-msp-pain-points/RESULT.md` with:

1. **10 typical small-business IT pain points.** One line each, ranked by how often they come up, each backed by a public source (surveys from SBA, CompTIA, Verizon DBIR, NCSC/CISA, Microsoft, vendor reports, etc.). Include a year for every survey figure.
2. **What local MSPs / IT shops in north-central WV charge.** A table: business name, town, service, published price or price model (per user/month, hourly, flat), and the public URL where that price appears. Only list prices that are actually published on a public page. If a shop does not publish prices, list it once with "not published". Aim for 5-10 shops. If too few WV shops publish prices, add a short second table of published prices from nearby regional MSPs (PA/OH/MD/VA) or national benchmarks, clearly labelled as not local.
3. **Three takeaways** for pricing a small new service, in plain words.

Rules: public sources only, a link for every fact, retrieval date for prices. Say "unsure" rather than guess. Keep it to about one printed page plus the sources list. End with **How I checked it** (which claims you confirmed on a second page) and **Unsure about**.

Delivery: this will be longer than one chat screen, so push `results/wv-msp-pain-points/RESULT.md` (commit message `result wv-msp-pain-points`, no build leftovers). Then in chat reply with three lines: `RESULT wv-msp-pain-points`, a one-line summary, `END wv-msp-pain-points`.
