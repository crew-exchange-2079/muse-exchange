# Fixer-upper: resale, neighbourhoods, land and DIY (north-central WV)

Follow-up to `results/fixer-upper-research` and `results/fixer-repair-costs`. Same buyer: a 1,500+ sq ft fixer-upper in Harrison, Marion, Monongalia, Lewis or Upshur County, WV, to live in, and maybe rent or sell in 3 to 5 years. Text only, no images. Write `results/fixer-area-land-diy/RESULT.md`:

1. **Resale and after-repair value:** recent sold-price ranges per sq ft for renovated vs. unrenovated houses in each county and its main towns; days on market; price trend over the last 3 years; how to estimate after-repair value and the "don't over-improve" ceiling per area.
2. **Neighbourhoods:** per county and main town: flood-zone exposure (FEMA maps), school ratings, broadband availability, commute times to the I-79 corridor, crime trend, and planned growth (hospital, industry, road projects). End with a short ranked list of towns or areas that look best for value plus resale.
3. **Land:** what to check on a WV lot: mineral rights severed or not (and how to look it up at the county clerk), mine subsidence, slope and drainage, well and septic vs. public water and sewer, perc tests, zoning for a garage or accessory unit, rights-of-way and gas lines. Typical costs to fix land problems.
4. **DIY vs. hire:** which renovation jobs an owner with a full-time job can realistically do (time and money saved, skill level), which legally need a licensed WV contractor or a permit, and how WV permits work in unincorporated vs. town areas.
5. **Later renting:** typical rents for a 3-bed in each county, WV landlord rules in brief, and whether house hacking (renting a room or unit) is common there.
6. **Running costs of an old house:** insurance problems (old wiring, roof age), WV property tax with the homestead exemption and reassessment after sale, heating fuel options and costs.

Public sources, dated links, ranges, mark uncertainty. No personal details.
