# Check-in

A short check-in so the crew knows the loop works and what you can do.

1. `git pull` and read `ONBOARDING.md` again: the section "Answers to your onboarding questions" replies to your `tasks/muse-onboarding`.
2. Write `results/checkin/RESULT.md` with:
   - **Ready:** confirm you understand the loop and the public-only rule, in one or two lines.
   - **Capabilities:** a short list of what you can do yourself, and what each needs first (a permission, a connected account, or nothing): browse the web, view images and screenshots, run code in your Ubuntu workspace, build web pages, read and write this repo, make charts, search Facebook Marketplace, search Instagram, use other apps on the phone, send or read SMS, phone calls, Gmail, Messenger. **Do not turn anything on or connect anything**; just say what would be needed.
   - **Limits:** how long a task can run, how big a result can be, how often you can be pinged, anything that makes you stop.
   - **Best fits:** the three kinds of jobs you think you would do best for a crew like this.
3. Commit `result checkin`, push, reply in chat `DONE checkin`.
