Inspection Add-ons Guide

A standard home inspection doesn't cover everything. In Appalachia, strongly consider these add-on inspections during your due diligence period.

  • Radon Testing ($150 - $250): Essential. The region has elevated radon risks due to rocky soil. Fall/winter is the best time for testing, but it can be done year-round.
  • Well Water Testing ($100 - $300): Test for coliform bacteria, nitrates, lead, and heavy metals. Standard inspectors do not evaluate water quality.
  • Septic Inspection ($300 - $600): Do not rely on a standard home inspector. Hire a dedicated septic company to pump the tank and inspect the baffles and drainfield.
  • Pest & Wood Destroying Insect (WDI) Inspection ($75 - $150): Crucial for older homes to check for termite or carpenter ant damage, especially in areas with wood siding or soil-to-wood contact.
  • Chimney Inspection (Level 2) ($200 - $400): Essential if the home has a fireplace or woodstove. Many older chimneys lack proper liners or have creosote buildup.
  • Structural Engineering Inspection ($400 - $800): If the home inspector notes significant foundation cracks, bowing walls, or sagging floors, get an engineer's opinion.

Home Maintenance Calendar

A month-by-month guide to keep your home running smoothly.

Spring

  • March: Test sump pump; check roof for winter damage; inspect gutters.
  • April: Clean gutters and downspouts; schedule AC service; inspect foundation grading; turn on exterior water.
  • May: Test well water; pump septic tank if due; clean dryer vent; inspect siding and trim.

Summer

  • June: Trim vegetation away from house/AC; check windows/doors for drafts; inspect deck/porch.
  • July: Deep clean appliances (vacuum fridge coils); flush water heater; check garage door sensors.
  • August: Clean bathroom exhaust fans; inspect driveway for cracks; clean window weep holes.

Fall

  • September: Schedule furnace service; inspect chimney/woodstove; seal air leaks (caulk/weatherstrip).
  • October: Clean gutters after leaf fall; winterize exterior plumbing; retest for radon; rake leaves from foundation.
  • November: Check attic insulation/drafts; clean kitchen exhaust hood; test GFCI outlets.

Winter

  • December: Close crawlspace vents to prevent freezing; test sump pump; replace furnace filter.
  • January: Clear snow from gas/dryer vents; replace furnace filter; check attic for ice dams; test smoke/CO detectors.
  • February: Inspect basement/crawlspace for moisture during thaws; check plumbing for leaks; replace furnace filter.
