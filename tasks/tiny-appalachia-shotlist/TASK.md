# "Tiny Appalachia" — a 30-clip shot list from real West Virginia places

A new short-video series shows real West Virginia places and moments as tilt-shift miniature dioramas (cozy, nostalgic, respectful; no stereotypes). Research real, recognisable public subjects and write `results/tiny-appalachia-shotlist/RESULT.md`:

1. **30 subjects** across the state: landmarks (bridges, courthouses, lighthouses-equivalents like fire towers, rail lines, dams, caverns, ski areas), small-town main streets, seasonal events (festivals, fairs), historic moments (rail, coal, glass, pottery, music), and nature scenes. For each: name, county, one line of why it is loved, the best season/time of day to depict, 2-3 visual details that make it instantly recognisable, and a public reference link (official site, Wikipedia, WV Tourism).
2. **A text-to-video prompt** for each in one consistent house style: tilt-shift miniature diorama, shallow depth of field, slow camera push or orbit, 6 seconds, vertical 9:16, soft natural light; include the recognisable details; avoid readable text and logos.
3. **A 30-day posting order** mixing regions and seasons (it is October: lean into fall first), with a one-line caption hook per day and 5 hashtags.
4. **Sound ideas** per clip that need no copyrighted music (ambient, field sounds, simple banjo/fiddle-style royalty-free cues), with sources of free-to-use audio.
Public sources only, dated links. No personal details.
